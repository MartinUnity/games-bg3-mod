   A list of spells available to be learned by the [40]Ranger class upon
   level up.

Overview[[41]edit section | [42]visual editor]

   [43][95px-Class_Ranger_Hotbar_Icon.png.webp]

   The level column indicates spell level, not class level. Ranger spells
   are [44]Known spells and cannot be changed after they are learned
   during through a level up.

   Ranger spells will use the caster's [45]Wisdom modifier for [46]Spell
   Save DC and [47]Spell Attack rolls.

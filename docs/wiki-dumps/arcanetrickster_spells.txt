   A lists of spells available to be prepared by the [40]Arcane Trickster
   subclass of [41]Rogue.

Overview[[42]edit section | [43]visual editor]

   [44][95px-Class_Rogue_Arcane_Trickster_Hotbar_Icon.png.webp]

   The spell list is the same as the Wizard's, but with only non-cantrip
   spells from the schools of [45]Enchantment or [46]Illusion plus every
   wizard cantrip. However, Arcane Tricksters may learn a spell from the
   entire wizard spell list at levels 3 and 8. The Replacement Spell
   Feature also allows an Arcane Trickster to swap any known spell with
   any spell from the full wizard spell list, once per level starting at
   class level 4.

   Arcane Trickster spells will use the caster's [47]Intelligence modifier
   for [48]Spell Save DC and [49]Spell Attack rolls.

   The following spell list is the limited version. For the full list of
   Wizard spells from every spell school, see the [50]Wizard Spell List.

   A list of spells available to be prepared by the [40]Cleric class.

Overview[[41]edit section | [42]visual editor]

   [43][95px-Class_Cleric_Hotbar_Icon.png.webp]

   Cleric spells are [44]Prepared spells, so all Clerics have access to
   these spells (up to their Cleric level) and prepare a limited number of
   them at any one time.

   Cleric spells use the caster's [45]Wisdom modifier for [46]Spell Save
   DC and [47]Spell Attack rolls.

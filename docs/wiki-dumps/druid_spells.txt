   A lists of spells available to be prepared by the [40]Druid class.

Overview[[41]edit section | [42]visual editor]

   [43][95px-Class_Druid_Hotbar_Icon.png.webp]

   Druid spells are [44]Prepared spells, so all Druids have access to
   these spells (up to their Druid level) and prepare a limited number of
   them at any one time.

   Druid spells will use the caster's [45]Wisdom modifier for [46]Spell
   Save DC and [47]Spell Attack rolls.

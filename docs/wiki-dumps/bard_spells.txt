   A list of spells available to be learned by the [40]Bard class upon
   level up.

Overview[[41]edit section | [42]visual editor]

   [43][95px-Class_Bard_Hotbar_Icon.png.webp]

   The level column indicates spell level, not class level. Bard spells
   are [44]Known spells and cannot be changed after they are learned
   during through a level up.

   Bard spells will use the caster's [45]Charisma modifier for [46]Spell
   Save DC and [47]Spell Attack rolls.

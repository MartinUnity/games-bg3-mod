   A list of spells available to be prepared by the [40]Paladin class.

Overview[[41]edit section | [42]visual editor]

   [43][95px-Class_Paladin_Hotbar_Icon.png.webp]

   Paladin spells are [44]Prepared spells, so all Paladins have access to
   these spells (up to their Paladin level) and prepare a limited number
   of them at any one time.

   Paladin spells will use the caster's [45]Charisma modifier for
   [46]Spell Save DC and [47]Spell Attack rolls.

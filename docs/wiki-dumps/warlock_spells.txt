   A list of spells available to be learned by the [40]Warlock class upon
   level up.

Overview[[41]edit section | [42]visual editor]

   [43][95px-Class_Warlock_Hotbar_Icon.png.webp]

   The level column indicates minimum Warlock level needed.

   Warlock spells are [44]Known spells and cannot be changed after they
   are learned through a level up.

   Warlock spells will use the caster's [45]Charisma modifier for
   [46]Spell Save DC and [47]Spell Attack rolls.

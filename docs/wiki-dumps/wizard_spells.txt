Overview[[51]edit section | [52]visual editor]

   [53][95px-Class_Wizard_Hotbar_Icon.png.webp]

   Wizard spells are [54]prepared spells, and must be prepared by the
   wizard out of the spells in their spellbook. Unlike other classes that
   use prepared spells, wizards don't gain access to all wizard spells
   automatically. They select 6 spells to learn initially, and can only
   learn an additional 2 on each level up. Outside of that, they must
   [55]transcribe spells into their spellbook by using [56]scrolls. In
   exchange, the wizard spell list is considerably larger than other
   classes.

   Wizard spells will use the caster's [57]Intelligence modifier for
   [58]Spell Save DC and [59]Spell Attack rolls.
